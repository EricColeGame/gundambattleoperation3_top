Waiting for a **Gundam Battle Operation 3 release date**? You are not the only one. The absence of a date is not a red flag. It simply means the marketing clock has not started. What you *can* plan around are the network tests, which are the strongest signal of how far along the game actually is.

## Network Tests: Your First Chance to Play Before Release

Two tests are on the table, and they are very different in scope.

The first is a Japan-only cross-play network test on **November 20 and November 22**. Each day runs three separate windows. The second is an open network test aimed at a wider pool of players, with no dates attached yet.

| Test | Dates | Daily windows | Region | Cross-play |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Closed cross-play test | November 20, 2026 | 09:00–12:00, 15:00–18:00, 21:00–24:00 | Japan only | Yes |
| Closed cross-play test | November 22, 2026 | 09:00–12:00, 15:00–18:00, 21:00–24:00 | Japan only | Yes |
| Open network test | TBA | TBA | Wider availability | Expected |

Two practical notes. First, the listed hours read as Japan local time, so convert them before blocking out an evening. Second, the three-window structure means each session is three hours — enough for a few matches, not a marathon.

Cross-play being tested this early is the detail to watch. If the November sessions go smoothly, the team has cleared one of the biggest technical hurdles standing between the tests and a launch announcement.

## What the Timeline Suggests About the Release Window

Nobody outside Bandai Namco knows the date, but the announcement cadence gives you something to reason with. Here is how the pieces line up.

| Signal | What it implies |
|---|---|
| Network test scheduled for November 2026 | Servers and matchmaking are functional but still being tuned |
| A second, larger open test promised | Launch is not imminent; the team wants scale testing first |
| Launch lineup locked to four series | Content is scoped, but the roster is still thin |
| Two to three Alternative series planned for year one | Post-launch support is planned before launch happens |
| No store pages or pre-registration mentioned | Marketing ramp-up has not started |

Read together, those signals point to a gap between the tests and launch. A title that ships weeks after its first network test usually has store pages, pre-load details, and a pre-order or pre-registration hook already live. None of that exists yet.

**Player experience** from previous *Battle Operation* cycles suggests Bandai Namco likes to run a test, gather feedback, run a second test, then announce a window. If that pattern holds, the **Gundam Battle Operation 3 release date** will likely be revealed sometime after the open test concludes — not before.

Treat any specific date circulating on social media as unverified until it appears on an official channel.

## How to Prepare Before the Gundam Battle Operation 3 Release Date Arrives

You cannot pre-order a free-to-play game, but you can set yourself up so launch week is not a scramble.

| Step | Why it matters | Effort |
|---|---|---|
| Follow the official channels in your language | Announcements dropped in six languages at once; date news will too | 5 minutes |
| Watch the announcement trailer for your region | Confirms terminology and suit names you will see in-game | 10 minutes |
| Check your PS5 or PC setup against modern free-to-play specs | Cross-play titles benefit from a stable wired connection | 20 minutes |
| Clear storage space now | Free-to-play installs grow fast with post-launch suits | 10 minutes |
| Join community hubs and discords | Test codes and open-test timing spread there first | 15 minutes |

A few habits worth building early:

- **Learn the rock-paper-scissors of raid, general, and support roles.** Team composition decides matches more than raw aim, and the announcement explicitly calls out balancing long-range firepower against close-quarters combat.
- **Practice resource discipline in *Battle Operation 2*.** The sequel will likely carry a similar economy, and players who understand upgrade costs adapt faster.
- **Try the Base Camp social features when the test opens.** The hub is designed for finding squads, and a premade team beats random matchmaking almost every time.
- **Do not burn savings on *Battle Operation 2* tokens right before a transition.** Community reports suggest populations shift hard when a sequel launches.

One more thing: if you are outside Japan, the November test will not be playable for you. Do not let that sour you on the game — the open network test is explicitly described as available to "even more players," which is the session most of the world will actually get into.

## FAQ

**Does Gundam Battle Operation 3 have a release date yet?**
No. As of October 29, 2026, Bandai Namco has confirmed the game for PS5 and PC but has not announced a launch day, season, or window. The **Gundam Battle Operation 3 release date** will likely be revealed after network testing concludes.

**Is Gundam Battle Operation 3 free-to-play?**
Yes. Bandai Namco describes it as the latest entry in the free-to-play team combat series, set in the *Mobile Suit Gundam* multiverse. You enlist as a mercenary, pilot mobile suits, and fight in large-scale online team battles.

**Which Gundam series are confirmed for launch?**
*Mobile Suit Gundam*, *The 08th MS Team*, *0083: Stardust Memory*, and *Mobile Suit Gundam SEED*. Two to three additional Alternative series are planned for the first year after release.

**Will Gundam Battle Operation 3 support cross-play?**
Cross-play is being tested directly — the November 20 and 22 network test in Japan is billed as a cross-play test. A broader open network test is also planned, though its dates and regional availability have not been announced.

**How do I get into the network test?**
The November sessions are Japan-only, so players elsewhere will need to wait for the open test. Watch official Bandai Namco channels and community hubs for sign-up details, which have not been published yet.
