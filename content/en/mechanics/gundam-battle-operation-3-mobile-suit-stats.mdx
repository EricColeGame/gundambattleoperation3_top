## The Core Stat Categories Behind Gundam Battle Operation 3 Mobile Suit Stats

The Battle Operation series has always used a fairly wide stat block, and the sequel is expected to carry that vocabulary forward with adjustments. Here's the working map of what each field does in practice.

| Stat | What it controls | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| HP | Total damage a unit can absorb | Determines how aggressively you can trade |
| Ballistic resistance | Damage reduction vs. shell/projectile weapons | Protects against machine guns, bazookas, most raids |
| Beam resistance | Damage reduction vs. beam weapons | Decides survivability against rifle-heavy teams |
| Ranged attack | Damage output of shooting weapons | Scales beam rifle and vulcan damage |
| Melee attack | Damage output of close-combat weapons | Scales saber and tackle damage |
| Mobility / speed | Movement and strafe velocity | Governs repositioning and dodge windows |
| Thruster capacity | Total boost gauge | Controls how long you can chain boosts |
| Turn speed | Rotation rate while moving | Affects close-range tracking and duels |
| Radar / sensor range | Detection radius | Shapes how early you see flankers |
| Skills | Passive modifiers | Often worth more than raw stats |

Two things trip people up immediately. First, resistances are not a single "defense" number — a unit can be a beam sponge and a ballistic paper bag at the same time, which is why mixed-damage teams beat single-type teams. Second, thruster capacity and turn speed rarely appear in flashy marketing but decide most close-range duels. A unit with modest attack values and generous boost can out-trade a glass cannon all day.

## How Role, Cost, and Environment Reshape Every Number

Raw Gundam Battle Operation 3 mobile suit stats are only half the story. The role triangle does the rest. In the series' established structure, Raid units beat Support, Support beats General, and General beats Raid, with a damage modifier applied on top of the base numbers. That means a "weaker" stat line in the right matchup can outperform a stronger one.

| Role | Stat priorities | Stats you can afford to sacrifice |
|---|---|---|
| Raid | Melee attack, mobility, thruster | Beam resistance, ranged attack |
| General | Balanced HP, both resistances, ranged attack | Peak melee output |
| Support | Ranged attack, HP, sensor range | Turn speed, melee attack |

Cost tier matters just as much. A high-cost unit with excellent stats is not automatically better than a low-cost unit with mediocre stats, because cost determines respawn economics and team composition limits. In practical terms, an efficient low-cost pick that survives the whole match contributes more than a high-cost unit that dies twice.

Environment is the third variable. Wide, open maps reward ranged attack and sensor range. Tight urban maps reward turn speed, boost capacity, and melee reach. Before you judge a stat line, ask which map pool you're queuing into.

## A Practical Method for Comparing Gundam Battle Operation 3 Mobile Suit Stats

When the full game and its network test data are available, use a repeatable workflow instead of eyeballing numbers.

| Step | Action | What you're looking for |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Read HP and both resistances together | Effective durability, not just raw HP |
| 2 | Divide attack by weapon cooldown | Damage per second, not damage per shot |
| 3 | Compare thruster capacity to boost consumption | Number of usable boost chains |
| 4 | Check turn speed against your playstyle | Duel comfort vs. ranged safety |
| 5 | Confirm role and cost fit your team | Matchup advantage, respawn cost |
| 6 | Test in a live match, not the stat screen | Real feel beats spreadsheets |

Step 6 is the one people skip. Stat screens cannot tell you how a unit *feels* — how much input lag the boost has, whether the melee animation commits you too long, or how forgiving the hitboxes are. Player experience consistently shows that comfort with a stat line beats theoretical optimization.

One more tip: build a small personal spreadsheet. Log HP, both resistances, attack values, thruster, and turn speed for the five units you actually play. After a dozen matches you'll see which stat you personally underuse, and that's the stat to prioritize when you unlock something new.

## Common Mistakes Players Make With Mobile Suit Stats

A few traps show up again and again in community reports and player experience threads:

- **Chasing the biggest attack number.** A 10% attack gain that costs you 20% thruster capacity is usually a net loss in real matches.
- **Ignoring resistances entirely.** Beam-only teams lose to beam-resistant walls; the fix is roster diversity, not more damage.
- **Treating cost as a power score.** Cost is an economy stat, not a quality rating.
- **Judging a unit before the balance patches land.** Live-service games rebalance constantly; early-tier lists age badly.
- **Copying a top player's loadout without their habits.** Their stat priorities reflect their aim, positioning, and reflexes, not yours.

Treat any early tier list — including this one — as a starting hypothesis. Gundam Battle Operation 3 mobile suit stats will shift with every balance pass, and the players who adapt fastest are the ones who understand *why* a number matters rather than memorizing which number is currently highest.

## FAQ

**Are Gundam Battle Operation 3 mobile suit stats officially published yet?**
No. As of late October 2026 the game has been reported for PS5 and PC, and a network test build has been spotted, but Bandai Namco has not released a complete stat database. Anything with precise per-unit values should be treated as unofficial.

**Will the stat system be the same as Battle Operation 2?**
Expect the same general categories — HP, resistances, attack, mobility, thruster, turn speed, skills — with tuning changes. Sequels in this series have historically kept the vocabulary while adjusting values and adding mechanics.

**Which stat should I prioritize as a new player?**
Thruster capacity and turn speed. They're invisible on a highlight reel but they determine whether you can escape, chase, and land melee hits. Once you're comfortable with movement, start optimizing attack and resistances.

**How often should I re-check Gundam Battle Operation 3 mobile suit stats after launch?**
After every balance patch and every new unit drop. In a live-service game, a stat sheet has a shelf life measured in weeks, not years — keep your own notes and update them as the meta moves.
