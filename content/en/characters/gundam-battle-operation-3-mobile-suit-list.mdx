### Why You Should Be Skeptical of "Leaked" Lists

Search for the **Gundam Battle Operation 3 mobile suit list** today and you'll find forum posts, AI-generated wiki pages, and clickbait slideshows. Community reports are worth watching, but treat any list without a Bandai Namco source as speculation. A useful rule: if a "leak" names exact costs, stats, and release dates for thirty units at once, it's fabricated. Real datamines arrive messy — internal IDs, placeholder names, missing models.

## What Battle Operation 2 Teaches Us About the Roster Formula

Battle Operation 2 launched on July 26, 2018, and has spent years adding units in waves. Its structure is the strongest predictor we have for how a **Gundam Battle Operation 3 mobile suit list** will be organized. Every unit falls into one of three combat types, and that triangle shapes every match.

| Type | Battlefield role | Strengths | Weaknesses | Best played as |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Raid | Aggressive flanker and assassin | High burst damage, mobility, type advantage over Support | Fragile, punished by Generals | A player who reads openings and commits |
| General | Frontline brawler | Balanced stats, durable, counters Raids | Struggles to crack Supports at range | The default pick for new pilots |
| Support | Fire support and area control | Long-range damage, stuns, counters Generals | Slow, vulnerable to Raids | A patient player who holds sightlines |

Beyond type, every unit sits in a cost bracket that gates it into match tiers. Cost roughly tracks era and power, so a low-cost grunt suit from the One Year War never shares a lobby with a late-UC ace machine unless the match rules allow it.

| Cost tier | Typical era | What to expect |
|---|---|---|
| Low cost | Early Universal Century | Simple kits, forgiving hitboxes, great for learning fundamentals |
| Mid cost | Gryps Conflict through ZZ | Transformation gimmicks, beam weapons, stronger stun options |
| High cost | Char's Counterattack and beyond | Fast, high-damage, heavy emphasis on positioning and cooldowns |
| Top cost | Late UC and premium ace units | Psycoframe abilities, funnel weapons, tiny margin for error |

This tiering is exactly why a **Gundam Battle Operation 3 mobile suit list** will never be a flat alphabetical catalog. It will be a matrix of type, cost, environment (ground or space), and weapon loadout.

| Preparation step | Why it matters | Time to spend |

One practical tip from **player experience** in Battle Operation 2: new pilots who main a single Support unit get hunted relentlessly. Rotating between a General for frontline reps and a Raid for flanking reps builds matchup awareness faster than grinding one machine to mastery.

### Reading a Reveal Trailer for Roster Clues

When the first proper trailer lands, watch for three things: which suits appear in the gameplay montage (likely launch units), which appear in a teaser stinger (likely post-launch), and which appear only as background wreckage (likely not playable). Studios are deliberate about this. A suit shown performing a unique animation — a funnel launch, a transformation, a beam saber clash — is almost always playable, because that animation had to be built.
