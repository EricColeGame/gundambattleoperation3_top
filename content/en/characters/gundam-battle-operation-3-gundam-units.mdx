## The Class Triangle Is the Real Meta

Every conversation about **Gundam Battle Operation 3 Gundam units** starts with the raid–general–support triangle. It has anchored the series for years, and it solves the problem most team games have: nobody wants to play the boring role. Here, the "boring" role hard-counters the flashy one.

| Class | Job on the team | Strong against | Watch out for |
|---|---|---|---|
| Raid | Flanks, dives, deletes support units | Support | General units hunting you |
| General | Frontline brawler, holds the line | Raid | Getting kited by Supports at range |
| Support | Ranged pressure, spotting, area denial | General | Raids appearing behind you |

The practical takeaway: a team of six all-Raid players loses to a balanced squad almost every time. Community reports from *Battle Operation 2* consistently show that composition discipline beats individual mechanical skill in close matches. If you are new, learn one unit from each class before you main anything.

### Why cost matters as much as class

Units are priced by cost, and a team has a budget. Bringing three top-cost machines leaves no room for respawns. A useful rule of thumb from the previous game: keep at least one or two cheaper units in your lineup so a bad early exchange does not end your match.

| Cost band | Typical feel | Best used for |
|---|---|---|
| Low | Fast respawns, limited armor, cheap pressure | Filling gaps, learning maps, soaking time |
| Mid | The competitive sweet spot | Most ranked and casual sorties |
| High | Strong stats, long respawn penalty | Anchoring a push you are confident about |
| Top | Match-defining, unforgiving | Closing out a game you are already winning |

## How to Evaluate a Unit Before You Spend Resources

New **Gundam Battle Operation 3 Gundam units** will arrive constantly, and the first wave is always the most expensive to chase. Read the stat sheet in this order and you will avoid most buyer's remorse.

- **Movement and boost capacity first.** Speed and boost gauge determine whether a unit can actually use its weapons.
- **Weapon loadout second.** Look for a stun option plus a damage option. Units with only one or the other are situational.
- **Skill list third.** Emergency evasion, maneuver armor, and damage-reduction skills change matchups more than raw HP.
- **Ground vs. space rating last.** A great ground unit with a poor space rating is a part-time purchase.

### Ground and space are different games

Space maps remove gravity, which changes boost management, jump behavior, and how melee approaches land. Historically, roughly a third of any roster performs noticeably better in one environment. Check the rating before you commit.

| Environment | What changes | Units that thrive |
|---|---|---|
| Ground | Gravity, cover, verticality, chokepoints | Balanced generals, brawling raids, artillery supports |
| Space | Free movement, no cover, longer sightlines | High-mobility raids, beam-focused supports |

## Building Your First Sortie Lineup

You get a hangar, not a single pick, so plan three units that cover each other's weaknesses. The structure below is a proven template from the previous game and translates cleanly to **Gundam Battle Operation 3 Gundam units** planning.

| Slot | Recommended pick | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| Primary | Mid-cost general | Flexible, survives mistakes, fits most budgets |
| Secondary | Low-cost raid or support | Counter-picks the enemy composition |
| Emergency | Cheap grunt unit | Lets you re-enter the fight without crippling the team |

Three habits separate strong players from unit collectors:

1. **Learn one map per unit.** Knowing where your boost runs out is worth more than a stat upgrade.
2. **Watch the respawn timer, not the kill feed.** Dying at the wrong moment costs your team the objective.
3. **Test in free practice before you spend tickets.** Every new banner tempts you; ten minutes in training saves a month of regret.

Also expect live balance patches. In *Battle Operation 2*, buffs and nerfs reshuffled the tier list regularly, and early-tier lists for a new game are almost always wrong within two months. Treat any "best unit" ranking published in launch week as a starting point, not gospel.
