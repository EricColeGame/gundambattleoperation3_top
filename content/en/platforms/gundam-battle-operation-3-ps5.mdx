### Preparation Checklist

- **Verify eligibility first.** The November test is region-locked to Japan, so do not rebuild your schedule around it if your account region does not qualify.
- **Pre-load early.** Network test clients usually go live for download before the first session opens, and last-minute downloads eat an entire play window.
- **Set up a wired connection.** Cross-play tests are as much about netcode as they are about gameplay, and Wi-Fi noise muddies your read on hit detection.
- **Test one suit thoroughly.** Three hours per window is not enough time to sample the whole roster. Pick a single suit and learn its full arsenal.
- **Report what breaks.** Feedback submitted during a network test genuinely shapes balance passes before launch.

## Tips for New Pilots Starting Out

- **Choose a role before you choose a suit.** Decide whether you want to anchor a lane, flank, or support, then pick the mobile suit that serves that job.
- **Learn one loadout to mastery.** Cooldown timers and weapon swap speed are where duels are won. Muscle memory beats variety early on.
- **Use Base Camp deliberately.** The hub exists to build squads. Finding two or three consistent teammates will improve your win rate more than any single upgrade.
- **Watch the trailer in your language.** The multilingual reveal is not just marketing — it confirms the game is shipping with broad localization, so menu and UI comprehension will not be a barrier.
- **Treat the open test as a demo, not a beta review.** Balance in a network test is rarely final.
